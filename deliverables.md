**Team Name:** Metanoia

**Project:** Diffusion-Based Reconstruction of Missing Architectural Floor Plan Components

**Project Description:** An engineer or architect may unknowingly miss the floor plan component during drawing and we should develop a diffusion based reconstruction model to correct it.

**Expected Deliverables:**
- [ ] 1. Floor Plan Dataset & Missing-Component Generation: Curate architectural floor plan data and create controlled missing-component cases involving walls, doors, windows, rooms and other structural elements. 

- [ ] 2. Diffusion-Based Reconstruction Model: Develop a diffusion-based model that reconstructs missing floor-plan components while preserving the surrounding architectural structure. 

- [ ] 3. Structure-Aware Reconstruction: Incorporate constraints to ensure reconstructed components maintain wall connectivity, room boundaries, alignment and architectural consistency. 

- [ ] 4. Component-Level Evaluation: Evaluate reconstruction using component detection accuracy and other related metrics. 

- [ ] 5. Robustness & Ablation Study: Test the model with different missing-component types, locations and missing-region sizes and analyze the contribution of structural constraints.
